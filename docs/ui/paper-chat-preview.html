Evidence before explanation

A reading workflow for verifiable research conversations

2. Evidence and interpretation

A useful research conversation keeps claims close to their evidence. The reader should be able to return to the exact passage without losing their place in the paper.

Every generated claim should remain connected to its source passage.

The interface distinguishes what the paper says from an interpretation offered by a model. A physical page reference provides a stable path back to the document.

2.1 A bounded reading task

Selection provides context for a question. It does not replace the complete text of the paper. When the document has no text layer, the reader needs OCR before discussing that content.

3. Discussion

Conversation belongs beside the document. A compact side pane makes room for explanation while keeping the original argument in view. Controls recede until a decision is necessary.

Returning to a citation should not create a permanent annotation. A temporary highlight indicates the evidence and then leaves the paper as it was.

3.1 Limits

This page and all conversations in this preview are synthetic design material. They do not report an experiment, measured model quality, or an actual search run.
